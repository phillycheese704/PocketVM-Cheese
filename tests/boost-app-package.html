You're the blue team. Score through the orange goal. Free play is a good place to learn, with unlimited boost.

Drive & reverseWASD or arrows. On touch, push the left stick up to drive, down to reverse.
Boost & refuelHold Shift / BOOST. Gold pads give 12 boost; large orbs fill the tank.
Jump & dodgeSpace / JUMP. Tap again within 1.5 seconds while steering to dodge, or release direction for a double jump.
Aerial controlWhile airborne, up tilts the nose up. Steer and boost to meet the ball.
Drift & demolishHold Q / SLIDE for tight turns. A fast boosted hit can knock an opponent out for three seconds.
Camera & controllerC toggles ball cam. Controller: left stick drive, RT throttle, LT reverse, A jump, B boost, X slide, Y camera.
