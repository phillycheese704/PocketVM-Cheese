# Stick War

An original browser adaptation inspired by the economy, army commands, unit roles and individual control of Stick War: Legacy. Original canvas artwork, animations, landscapes, interface and synthesised audio; no game assets or music are copied.

## Play

Install **Stick War** from the PocketVM Store. It installs on the virtual drive, works offline, opens maximised and can be pinned to the desktop. Campaign stars, armoury upgrades, settings and survival records persist through uninstall/reinstall. A running battle is not saved after closing the game.

- **Campaign:** twelve territories; progressively unlock archers, spearmen, mages and giants. Hold missions require surviving until dawn. The final enemy statue awakens a heavy guardian when damaged.
- **Armoury:** earn two points on each territory's first victory. Eight upgrade paths have three permanent levels each. Replaying missions can improve stars but cannot farm duplicate points.
- **Skirmish:** all six units, four landscapes, easy/normal/hard/random enemy difficulty and random terrain.
- **Endless Deads:** escalating waves and heavy assaults every fifth wave. Record saves when your statue falls.

## Economy and combat

Miners carry gold from the mine to the statue; delivery makes it available. A small statue income keeps recovery possible. Training pays gold once, reserves population, takes time and can be cancelled for a refund. The army limit is 36 population; larger units consume more. AI uses paid training, miners and the same queue and population limit. Campaign starting armies, resources and reserve reinforcements vary by mission.

Swordsmen pressure archers. Spearmen resist frontal attacks. Archers outrange mages but do less damage to statues. Mages stun crowds and summon temporary minions. Giants stomp nearby formations. Garrison retreats, heals soldiers and activates defensive archers. Frost slows both armies equally. Costs and timing are tuned for this adaptation, not claimed to match the original game's exact numbers.

## Controls

Touch or click the bottom unit cards to train. **Defend / Attack / Garrison** issue army orders. Tap a friendly unit to control it; movement uses the touch joystick or WASD/arrows, attacks use Attack/Space, and guarding uses Block/Q. Controlled miners work faster, but you must move them back to the statue to deposit a full load. E or the special button throws a spearman’s spear once or summons a mage’s minions on cooldown. Release/R returns a unit to automatic orders. Drag the battlefield, tap the minimap or use Home/Front to move the camera. Keys 1–6 train units; Z/X/C choose commands; Escape pauses. Leaving the tab pauses the battle and releases held inputs. Battle speed is 1× or 2×.
