# Footy Physics

Original 2D hinged-leg football inspired by Soccer Physics. Solo versus three AI levels, two- and four-player local multiplayer, one-button or separate-player controls. First to 3/5/7/10, eight optional random round twists, earned cosmetic kits, career stats and endless practice. Original stadium art and synthesized audio.

Blue: Up/Space; red: W. Separate-player mode: blue Left/Right; red A/D. Hold to hop. Big touch buttons support independent fingers. Pause on blur or rotation.

Rigid bodies with SAT/circle contacts, restitution, friction, angular impulses and hinged leg constraints. Random rounds never repeat consecutively. Goal debounce, net crossbars and automatic ball nudge avoid deadlocks. Three downloadable mods change real gravity, ball geometry and hop impulses. Native offline Store package, source-checked sanitized saves and isolated modded progress. Reference: https://ottoojala.itch.io/soccer-physics.
