# Bouncemasters

Original PocketVM penguin-launch arcade inspired by the Famobi browser Bouncemasters format linked by the user. Tap to drop, tap near bat height to launch, then time animal bumps while descending. Contact taps are briefly buffered and only boost close to an animal. Momentum, gravity, snow restitution, speed limits and skidding are simulated at 120 Hz. Pads follow the drag-adjusted landing trajectory at every upgrade level. Coins/gems/chests use swept pickup checks. Course objects are removed behind the camera.

Three winter regions rotate endlessly. Four upgrade paths: Power, Max Speed, Rebound and Beat Force; the first Beat Force purchase costs800 coins. Cumulative route distance awards trophy milestones. Daily gift is 150 coins +1 gem once per UTC date. Five cosmetic bats and four outfits are purchased with earned gems. Each preview uses the same drawing as its in-game model. No ads, purchases, online leaderboard or accounts.

Tap/click or Space performs the flight action. One big touch button, timing gauge and clear animal-ready cue. Relaxed timing, pause on blur/rotation, reduced particles and synthesized effects. Practice never changes career rewards. Completed earnings and milestones save once; the route map and options remain local.

Three native mods change gravity, pickup amounts or collection radius, with isolated save keys. Offline Store packages embed all JavaScript, verify exact bytes and source-check/sanitize saves. Original art and code. Reference: https://www.crazygames.com/game/bouncemasters.
