# Avenge FPS

Original PocketVM hero-arena FPS inspired by the objective, loadout and spell-card format of Venge.io. Single-player against AI, no online multiplayer or accounts. Four heroes have main and secondary abilities; spells imbue main ability hits. Earn tier XP by eliminations and objectives. Four maps, four modes, eight weapons, three difficulty levels and practice.

WASD movement, Space jump, click/Q fire, right-click/Shift aim, F main ability, E secondary ability, R reload, 1–4/wheel switch weapon, B spell deck. On touch: independent joystick, drag to look, fire, aim, jump, reload, ability, secondary and switch controls. Pause on blur and orientation changes.

Store packages embed all JavaScript and the licensed Three.js runtime offline. Career saves are source-checked and sanitized by PocketVM. Three downloadable mods use isolated saves. Original procedural low-poly art. Reference: official Venge Social Club wiki https://social.venge.io/ (hero abilities, loadouts and modes).
