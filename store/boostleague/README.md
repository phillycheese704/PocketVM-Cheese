# Boost League

An original PocketVM car football game inspired by Rocket League. A 3D perspective stadium, fixed-step physical ball, momentum-driven cars, banked walls, boost pickups, aerials, double jumps, directional dodges and demolitions.

Exhibition: 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3 against local AI. Rookie, Pro and All-star; three stadiums; 2, 3 or 5 minutes; Random options; a grounded ball ends regulation and tied matches enter sudden-death overtime. Free play has one car, unlimited boost and a Reset ball button.

Garage: three body styles with a shared fair hitbox, custom paint and three decals. Preferences and career statistics save locally and through the PocketVM sandbox protocol. The installed package is self-contained and works offline; effects are synthesized locally.

Controls: WASD / arrows to drive, Shift boost, Space jump (again for double jump or directional dodge), Q powerslide, C camera, Esc pause, R reset in free play. Touch uses an independent left drive stick and large Slide, Jump and Boost buttons. Gamepad uses left stick or triggers to drive, A jump, B boost, X slide, Y camera and Start pause.

This is a local game with AI opponents, original artwork and a custom engine. It does not connect to Rocket League services.
