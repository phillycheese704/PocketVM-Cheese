FIELD GUIDE

A good night's plan.

Swap adjacent tiles to make lines of three. Unsuccessful swaps cost nothing. Cascades collect every matched tile. Daylight lasts 12 successful swaps; you can think as long as you like.

At night, time runs continuously. Wood equips shields, stone equips swords, and the other tiles call creatures. Match equipment before summoning a crowd. The survivor attacks nearby creatures automatically; empty swords mean no attack.

Heal with potions, bomb a crowd or spend tower mana to freeze time. Every fifth night has a boss: defeat it before dawn. Upgrades improve equipment and unlock new resources. Survive 25 nights, then continue endlessly.
