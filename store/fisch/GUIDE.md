# Fisch: Horizon

An original single-player PocketVM game inspired by the fishing and exploration loop of [Fisch on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/16732694052/Fisch). It runs in the browser; it is not a Roblox experience and does not connect to Roblox accounts.

Start at Willow Harbor's dock. Hold Space or the large fishing button and release in the green zone. Wait for a bite, tapping to shake if you want it sooner. While reeling, hold to move your mint control bar right and release to move left. Keep the gold fish marker inside it until progress reaches 100%. A perfect cast adds 15% to catch value. X cancels a line.

WASD or arrows move. Drag the world to rotate the camera; scroll to zoom. Left Shift sprints, Right Shift jumps. I opens your creel, J the bestiary, M the chart, R the outfitters, Q quests, and Escape settings. On touch screens, use the joystick, drag the open world with another finger, and use the large fishing, Boat, Jump and Trade buttons. Touch controls can be forced on in Settings. The camera and joystick use separate pointers.

Summon the free Harbor Skiff with B or Boat beside any dock. W/S accelerate and reverse; A/D steer. The touch joystick combines throttle and steering. Stop near a dock and press Boat to disembark. Islands become fast-travel destinations when discovered. The chart sets a minimap destination. Return to nearest dock in Settings can recover you from a difficult position.

Six original islands contain 60 fish species, each with a favourite bait, rarity, weight, value and fishing conditions. Some need night, rain, storm, fog or aurora. The bestiary reveals useful conditions even before discovery. Weather changes every three minutes of active play and a day takes twelve minutes. Menus, hidden tabs and unfocused windows pause the simulation.

Sell unlocked catches at a port. Lock favourites before selling all. The creel holds 240 fish. Eight rods vary in luck, control, resilience, speed and weight allowance. Buy ten-cast bait packs, three boats and four selectable enchantments. Find relics in catches, treasure chests and the daily quest. The enchantment applies across your equipped rods, and a new enchantment replaces the previous one. Discover 35 different species to earn the Horizon Rod.

Twelve permanent quests and an eight-catch daily quest award coins and XP. Claim each reward in the quest journal. Daily quests reset by UTC date. Every island has one treasure chest. Five catch variants (normal, shiny, golden, lunar and abyssal) add collection variety and value. Gentle Fishing, enabled initially, widens the reel bar by seven percentage points; disable it for a tighter challenge.

Progress saves in this browser and bridges into the PocketVM shell when installed. Saves include fish, gear, discoveries, rewards, preferences, time, weather and position. An active unfinished cast is not restored. There is no multiplayer or cloud save. Uninstalling the game keeps the profile; PocketVM's profile erase clears it.

All models, icons, fish illustrations, interface and sounds are original procedural work. Three.js is vendored under the included MIT licence. The perspective Canvas renderer provides the same world and simulation when WebGL is unavailable. Desktop and simulated touch layouts are verified; physical device compatibility can vary.
